1.0.30 validation

Full npm test suite passed, including the new tests/manual-selection.mjs.
node --check index.mjs passed. Independent review reproduced the former false
STOPPED state and verified cancellation at metadata and PCM/lead boundaries.

The new tests execute the actual pinned Player and production M1S subclass:
- obsolete title lookup resolving before or after the new selection;
- obsolete title lookup rejecting while the current song is already ready;
- older play blocked on group preparation, PCM readiness or transport lead;
- old initial transport confirmation after a new selection reused that session;
- late HA reply only closes its abandoned local session, not the new one;
- explicit normal YouTube Stop invalidates pending title preparation;
- current validation and HA startup failures still publish STOPPED normally.
External title, HA and PCM readiness events are controlled in these tests.
They do not constitute a physical phone/hub playback test or remote HA ordering
verification.

Exact comparison with the 1.0.29 archive confirms the entire PCM session class,
PCM constants and buffers, source spawn/decode and prepared-source command path,
FIFO, final period handling, output loop and pacing remain byte-identical.
waitForTransportStarted and transport lead measurement are also identical.
No integration, per-track HA restart, new buffer or timing offset is introduced.
Production changes are limited to stale play lifecycle cancellation and guards.

Physical end-of-song clipping followed by silence remains unresolved.
A read-only audit of local integration source 0.37.20 found an existing individual
source-rebuffer path that clears pending PCM and kills the remote receiver on
HA PCM starvation. Occurrence at the user's cut has NOT been established.
The installed integration version is not verified. Existing integration logs
(single rebuffer started/ended) were requested before changing any transport.
No integration source files were modified.

Publish sources and rebuild/update the add-on image. Confirm version 1.0.30.
